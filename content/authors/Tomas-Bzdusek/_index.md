I am an assistant professor at the [Physics Institute](https://www.physik.uzh.ch/en.html) of the University of Zurich, where I lead the research group [Theory of Topological Matter](https://www.physik.uzh.ch/en/groups/bzdusek.html). We are condensed matter theorists, and the team investigates diverse aspects of topological phases of matter. Much of my recent work applies the ideas of homotopy theory to classify the topology of single-particle Hamiltonians, with applications to topological insulators, semimetals, and superconductors, as well as to dissipative (non-Hermitian) and periodically driven (Floquet) systems. More recently, we have also ventured into the investigation of spectra and topological aspects of hyperbolic lattices.

I obtained my undergraduate degrees at [Comenius University](https://fmph.uniba.sk/en/) in Bratislava under the supervision of Richard Hlubina, and I did my doctoral studies at the Department of Physics of [ETH Zurich](https://www.phys.ethz.ch/) in the group of Manfred Sigrist. Afterwards, I spent two years as the Gordon and Betty Moore Fellow at the Geballe Laboratory for Advanced Materials at [Stanford University](https://glam.stanford.edu/), and four years as an Ambizione Fellow in the [CMT group](https://www.psi.ch/en/lsm/cmt-group) at the [Paul Scherrer Institute](https://www.psi.ch/en) in Villigen, Switzerland. During the latter appointment, I was also affiliated as an Associated Senior Scientist with the [group](https://www.physik.uzh.ch/en/groups/neupert/team.html) of Prof. Titus Neupert at the University of Zurich.
